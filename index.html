How It Works

From classroom graduation to job interview in 3 simple steps.

Step 1

The School Issues

The school or hackathon organizer fills in the student's name, course, and date. The app converts those details into a permanent, tamper-proof digital stamp.

Step 2

The Student Gets Proof

The student receives their official certificate with a verifiable QR code and unique link to showcase on their LinkedIn profile or resume.

Step 3

Anyone Instantly Verifies

An employer enters the Certificate ID or uploads the document. The app shows an instant green checkmark confirming "100% Real" or a red warning if altered.
